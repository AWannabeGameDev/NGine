Tile-layer : [tileset, [tile]] + [[tile reference]]
where tile is just ModelData and tileset is the tileset-image.

However, for a tile-layer, the input is a series of gids. Tilesets are stored separately. For each gid, we can binary-search the list of tilesets and store.

Rendering is super simple, each (tileset, [tile]) pair directly maps to one draw call
